**Principle: lead with the demo, not the pitch.** A skeptical contractor won't read a paragraph about software, but they'll click a 20-second calculator built for their trade. Keep it short, one CTA, personalize `{Name}`, `{Company}`, `{trade}`.

## 4) Loom / video walkthrough script (~45–60 sec)
- **(0–5s)** "Hey {Name} — 45 seconds, I want to show you something for {Company}."
- **(5–25s, screen-share the demo)** "This is a sample roll-off site. The customer picks a size… number of days… an add-on… and boom — instant estimate, $385. No phone call needed."
- **(25–40s, hit submit)** "When they submit, *you* get the lead — name, phone, email, and exactly what they wanted — in your dashboard and inbox."
- **(40–55s)** "That's every visitor who'd normally leave without calling. I can put one on your site in an afternoon, in your branding. Want me to set it up? Reply here or grab 15 minutes below."

## Where to send these
- **Email** — best for reach; find owners via county dumpster-permit lists, Google Maps listings, trade directories.
- **Facebook/Instagram DM** — contractors live here; the demo link travels well.
- **In person** — supply houses, dump/transfer stations, local trade groups; pair with the printed one-pager (QR → demo).
- **Local trade Facebook groups** — offer the demo as a helpful tool, not a hard sell.

## Rules of thumb
1. One CTA per message. Don't ask them to "learn more" *and* "book a call" *and* "sign up."
2. Personalize the trade word and company name — generic = ignored.
3. Lead with the link. The demo does the selling.
4. Follow up once or twice, then stop. Short and respectful.
5. Track which channel converts (the demo + embed links are UTM-tagged — watch GA).
